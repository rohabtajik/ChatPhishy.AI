WELCOME TO AUSDAIS

Solving real-world data challenges through innovation and analytics

With roots in both academia and research communities, AusDAIS has always been devoted to simplifying data, sharing knowledge, and pursuing innovation. Founded in Australia, AusDAIS brings together data engineering, science, and analytics to solve present and future challenges.

We are a Melbourne-based Data Analytics hub with a passion for innovation and solving complex real-world data challenges. AusDAIS experts have accomplished various real-world data challenge projects. Our aim is to prepare future talents and improve their skills for future industry challenges.
